Run tsteps steps of an explicit 3-D heat equation stencil on the interior points 1 <= i, j, k <= n-2. Each step computes B = A + 0.125 (D_i A + D_j A + D_k A), where D_i A = A_{i+1,j,k} - 2 A_{i,j,k} + A_{i-1,j,k} and likewise for j and k, then the same update from B back into A. Boundary elements are never written.
