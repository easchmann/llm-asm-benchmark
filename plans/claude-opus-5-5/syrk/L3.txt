Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, m in rsi, C in rdx, A in rcx; alpha in xmm0, beta in xmm1. Nothing on the stack.
- rbx is callee-saved, pushed at entry and popped at the end; it holds m & -4.
- r9 points at the current row of C (starts at C, advances by 8*n); after that rdx is reused as the row index i.
- r8 points at row i of A (advances by 8*m per i), r10 at row j of A (starts at A for every i, advances by 8*m), r11 is j, rax is k.
- ymm2 is the vector accumulator, ymm3 a temporary; xmm0 and xmm1 stay alpha and beta.

Loops
1. For each row i from 0 while i < n:
   For j from 0 while j <= i (lower triangle including the diagonal):
   - Dot product d = sum over k of A[i][k] * A[j][k] (both rows are contiguous): vector part k < rbx step 4 into ymm2, reduce to a scalar, scalar part k < m.
   - C[i][j] = beta * C[i][j] + alpha * d (C row + 8*j).
   - Advance the row-j pointer by 8*m.
   Advance the C row by 8*n and the row-i pointer by 8*m.
2. Pop rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Edge cases
- Entries with j > i are never read or written.
- m < 4: vector part skipped; m not a multiple of 4: scalar tail. n = 1: only C[0][0].
- Unaligned vector loads.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add; the dot product is summed in four lanes.
