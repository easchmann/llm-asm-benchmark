Registers (System V)
- ni arrives in rdi, nj in rsi, nk in rdx, nl in rcx, tmp in r8, A in r9; B, C and D are on the stack. alpha arrives in xmm0, beta in xmm1.
- Callee-saved rbx, rbp, r12, r13, r14, r15 are pushed at entry (48 bytes), so B, C and D are then at 56(%rsp), 64(%rsp) and 72(%rsp); load them into r10 (B), r11 (C) and r12 (D). Pop in reverse order before returning.
- Phase 1 (tmp = beta * A * B): rbx is i, r13 points at row i of tmp (advances 8*nj), r14 at row i of A (advances 8*nk), rbp is k, r15 points at row k of B (advances 8*nj), rax is j, r9 (A is no longer needed once r14 is set) holds nj & -4.
- Phase 2 (D = beta * D + tmp * C): rbx is i, r13 points at row i of tmp, r14 at row i of D (advances 8*nl), rbp is j, r15 points at row j of C (advances 8*nl), rax is the column index l, r9 holds nl & -4.
- xmm0 (alpha) and xmm1 (beta) stay unchanged; ymm3 holds beta broadcast; ymm2 a broadcast scalar, ymm4, ymm5 temporaries.

Loops
1. Phase 1, for each row i of tmp:
   - Set the tmp row to zero (vector and scalar parts over nj), since tmp has undefined contents.
   - For k from 0 while k < nk: s = beta * A[i][k]; broadcast; tmp[i][j] += s * B[k][j] over j (vector part j < nj & -4 step 4, scalar tail).
2. Phase 2, for each row i of D:
   - Scale the D row by beta (vector and scalar parts over nl).
   - For j from 0 while j < nj: broadcast tmp[i][j]; D[i][l] += tmp[i][j] * C[j][l] over l (vector part l < nl & -4, scalar tail).
3. Pop the registers, vzeroupper, return.

Edge cases
- All sizes may be 1; vector parts are skipped whenever the row length is below 4, and scalar tails handle lengths that are not a multiple of 4.
- No element beyond the end of any row is accessed; row strides are 8*nj (tmp, B), 8*nk (A) and 8*nl (C, D) bytes.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add. Every output element receives its products in increasing order of the summation index.
