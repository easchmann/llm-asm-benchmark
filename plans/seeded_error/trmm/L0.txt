Registers (System V)
- m arrives in rdi, n in rsi, A in rdx, B in rcx; alpha in xmm0. Nothing on the stack.
- rbx and r12 are callee-saved, pushed at entry and popped at the end. rbx is the column index j, r12 = n & -4.
- r9 is the row index i, r8 points at row i of B (B + 8*n*i).
- r11 is k, r10 points at row k of B, rax points at A[i][k] (A + 8*(i*m + k)), which advances by 8 per k.
- ymm0 holds alpha broadcast (xmm0 low lane stays alpha), ymm2 the broadcast of A[i][k], ymm3, ymm4 temporaries.

Loops
Result row i = alpha * (B row i + sum over k > i of A[i][k] * B row k). Rows are processed in increasing i: row i only needs rows k > i, which have not been overwritten yet, so the update can be done in place.
1. For each i from 0 while i < m:
   a. For k from i+1 while k < m: broadcast A[i][k]; B[i][j] += A[i][k] * B[k][j] for all j (vector part j < r12 step 4, scalar part j < n).
   b. Scale row i by alpha (vector and scalar parts).
   c. Advance to the next row.
2. Pop r12 and rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Edge cases
- The diagonal of A is taken as 1 (row i itself is added with factor 1, not multiplied by A[i][i]); entries A[i][k] with k <= i are never read.
- i = m-1: no k loop iterations, the row is only scaled. m = 1: B = alpha * B.
- n < 4 or not a multiple of 4: scalar tails.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add. Each element receives the products in increasing k.
