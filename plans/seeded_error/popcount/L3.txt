Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, a in rsi, counts in rdx. Nothing on the stack.
- rdi is used as the count of remaining elements; rsi and rdx are advanced by 8 bytes per element, so no index register is needed.
- rax holds the current value, rcx is a temporary.
- r8, r9, r10, r11 hold the 64-bit masks 0x5555555555555555, 0x3333333333333333, 0x0F0F0F0F0F0F0F0F and the multiplier 0x0101010101010101 (each loaded once with a 64-bit immediate move).
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Loops
1. For each element (while rdi > 0):
   - load v = a[i] (8 bytes at rsi);
   - v = v - ((v >> 1) & 0x5555...)          (2-bit counts, logical shift)
   - v = (v & 0x3333...) + ((v >> 2) & 0x3333...)   (4-bit counts)
   - v = (v + (v >> 4)) & 0x0F0F...        (8-bit counts)
   - v = (v * 0x0101...) & 0xFF            (64-bit multiply, keep the low 64 bits; the low byte is the total)
   - store v to counts[i] (8 bytes at rdx); advance rsi and rdx by 8, decrement rdi.
2. Return.

Edge cases
- n = 1: one iteration. All 64 bits are counted, including the top bit, so all shifts are logical (unsigned).
- The count is between 0 and 64 and is stored as a full 64-bit value.

Vector instructions
- None. The bit counting uses the shift-and-mask method in general-purpose registers, because the POPCNT instruction is not part of SSE2, AVX or AVX2.
