Registers (System V)
- ni arrives in rdi, nj in rsi, nk in rdx, nl in rcx, nm in r8, E in r9; A, B, F, C, D and G are on the stack at 8, 16, 24, 32, 40 and 48 bytes above rsp on entry.
- Callee-saved rbx, rbp, r12, r13, r14, r15 are pushed at entry (48 bytes), so A, B, F, C, D, G are then at 56, 64, 72, 80, 88 and 96(%rsp). Pop in reverse order before returning.
- The sizes stay in rdi, rsi, rdx, rcx, r8 throughout and E stays in r9.
- Each of the three products X = Y * Z (X is p x q, Y is p x r, Z is r x q) uses the same loop nest and registers: rbx is the row index i, r13 points at row i of X (advances 8*q), r14 at row i of Y (advances 8*r), rbp is the inner index, r15 points at the current row of Z (starts at Z for every i, advances 8*q), r12 holds the start of Z, rax is the column index, r10 = q & -4.
- ymm0 is the broadcast of Y[i][k], ymm1, ymm2 temporaries.

Loops
1. Three products, in this order:
   a. E = A * B: p = ni, r = nk, q = nj.
   b. F = C * D: p = nj, r = nm, q = nl.
   c. G = E * F: p = ni, r = nj, q = nl.
   For each product, for each row i:
   - For E and F, set row i of X to zero (vector and scalar parts over q), because they have undefined contents; G is accumulated onto its initial contents.
   - For k from 0 while k < r: broadcast Y[i][k]; X[i][j] += Y[i][k] * Z[k][j] for all j (vector part j < r10 step 4, scalar tail).
2. Pop the registers, vzeroupper, return.

Edge cases
- Any size may be 1; vector parts are skipped for rows shorter than 4, scalar tails handle lengths that are not a multiple of 4.
- The third product must start only after E and F are complete.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add; each output element accumulates its products in increasing k.
