Registers (System V)
- ni arrives in rdi, nj in rsi, nk in rdx, C in rcx, A in r8, B in r9; alpha in xmm0, beta in xmm1. Nothing on the stack.
- rbx is callee-saved; it is pushed at entry and popped before returning. It holds nj & -4, the end of the vector part over columns.
- rdi counts the remaining rows of C. rcx points at the current row of C (advances by 8*nj), r8 at the current row of A (advances by 8*nk).
- r11 is k, r10 points at row k of B (starts at B, advances by 8*nj per k), rax is the column index j.
- ymm5 holds beta broadcast to four lanes; xmm0 (alpha) and xmm1 (beta) stay unchanged for the scalar parts. ymm2 holds the broadcast of alpha * A[i][k]; ymm3, ymm4 are temporaries.

Loops
1. For each row i of C:
   a. For k from 0 while k < nk: s = alpha * A[i][k] (A row + 8*k); broadcast s; row k of B at r10.
      Vector part: C[i][j..j+3] += s * B[k][j..j+3]; scalar part for the remaining columns. Advance r10 by 8*nj.
   b. Scale the row by beta: vector part j < rbx step 4 (C row + 8*j), scalar part j < nj.
   c. Advance the C row by 8*nj and the A row by 8*nk.
2. Pop rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Edge cases
- nj < 4: vector parts skipped. nj not a multiple of 4: the scalar parts handle the last columns.
- ni, nj or nk equal to 1 work through the same loops (single iterations).
- Each C[i][j] receives the products in increasing k, the same order as a straightforward triple loop.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add (no FMA).
