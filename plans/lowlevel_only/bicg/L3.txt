Registers (System V)
- m arrives in rdi, n in rsi, A in rdx, s in rcx, q in r8, p in r9; r is the seventh argument, on the stack at 8(%rsp), loaded into r10 at entry.
- rdx can serve as the row pointer of A (row stride 8*m bytes); rsi as a row counter; r10 (r) and r8 (q) advance by 8 bytes per row.
- rax holds the column index, r11 = m & -4.
- ymm2 is a vector accumulator, ymm3 holds r[i] broadcast, ymm4, ymm5 temporaries.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Memory and tails
- A[i][j] is at A + 8*(i*m + j).
- Vector parts cover columns below m & -4; scalar instructions handle the tail. m < 4: vector parts skipped.
- A vector accumulator is reduced to a scalar (upper 128 bits onto lower, then the two lanes).
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- vzeroupper before returning.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add.
