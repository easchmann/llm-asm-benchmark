Registers (System V)
- tsteps arrives in rdi, n in rsi, A in rdx, B in rcx. Nothing on the stack.
- rdx and rcx can serve as source and destination pointers.
- Callee-saved rbx, rbp, r12, r13, r14, r15 are pushed at entry and popped before returning.
- r15 = 8*n*n (bytes per i-plane). r8 holds i, r9 j, r10 k, r11 the end of the vector part over k, 1 + ((n - 2) & -4).
- Line pointers: rax points at the source line (i, j, 0), at source + 8*((i*n + j)*n); r14 at the destination line at the same offset; rbx = rax - r15 (line i-1), rbp = rax + r15 (line i+1), r12 = rax - 8*n (line j-1), r13 = rax + 8*n (line j+1).
- ymm15 holds 0.125 broadcast (from .rodata); ymm0-ymm5 are temporaries.

Memory and tails
- Element k of a line is at line pointer + 8*k; the k neighbours are at -8 and +8 bytes.
- The vector part along k ends at 1 + ((n - 2) & -4); scalar instructions handle the rest. n = 3: scalar only.
- The vector part along k reads at most up to index n-1 of the line.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- Pop the saved registers, vzeroupper, return.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate add, subtract and multiply, in the same order in the vector and scalar parts. One multiplication by 0.125 per point.
