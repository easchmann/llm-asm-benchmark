Registers (System V)
- ni arrives in rdi, nj in rsi, nk in rdx, C in rcx, A in r8, B in r9; alpha in xmm0, beta in xmm1. Nothing on the stack.
- rbx is callee-saved; it is pushed at entry and popped before returning. It holds nj & -4, the end of the vector part over columns.
- rdi can count rows of C. rcx can point at the current row of C (stride 8*nj bytes), r8 at the current row of A (stride 8*nk bytes).
- r11 holds k, r10 points at row k of B (stride 8*nj bytes), rax holds the column index.
- ymm5 holds beta broadcast to four lanes; xmm0 (alpha) and xmm1 (beta) stay unchanged for the scalar parts. ymm2 holds a broadcast scalar; ymm3, ymm4 are temporaries.

Memory and tails
- Vector parts cover columns below nj & -4; scalar parts handle the last columns. nj < 4: vector parts skipped.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- Pop rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add (no FMA).
