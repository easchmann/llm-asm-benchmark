Registers (System V)
- tsteps arrives in rdi, n in rsi, A in rdx, B in rcx. Nothing on the stack.
- rdx and rcx can serve as source and destination pointers.
- rax holds the index i, r8 the end of the vector part, 1 + ((n - 2) & -4), and r9 = n - 1.
- ymm2 holds 3.0 broadcast to four lanes (loaded from a constant in .rodata); ymm0, ymm1 are temporaries.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Memory and tails
- Element i is at base + 8*i; neighbours at -8 and +8 bytes, so three overlapping unaligned vector loads give src[i-1..i+2], src[i..i+3], src[i+1..i+4].
- The vector part ends at 1 + ((n - 2) & -4); scalar instructions handle the remaining elements. n = 3: scalar only.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- vzeroupper before returning.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit; separate adds followed by a division, the same operations as the scalar instructions, so vector and scalar results are identical.
