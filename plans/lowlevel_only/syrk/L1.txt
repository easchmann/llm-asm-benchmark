Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, m in rsi, C in rdx, A in rcx; alpha in xmm0, beta in xmm1. Nothing on the stack.
- rbx is callee-saved, pushed at entry and popped at the end; it holds m & -4.
- r9 can point at the current row of C (stride 8*n bytes), freeing rdx for a row index.
- r8 and r10 can point at rows of A (stride 8*m bytes), r11 and rax are indices.
- ymm2 is a vector accumulator, ymm3 a temporary; xmm0 and xmm1 stay alpha and beta.

Memory and tails
- C[i][j] is at C + 8*(i*n + j), A[i][k] at A + 8*(i*m + k).
- Vector parts cover k below m & -4; scalar tails handle the rest. m < 4: vector part skipped.
- The accumulator is reduced to a scalar.
- Unaligned vector loads.
- Pop rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add; four-lane partial sums.
