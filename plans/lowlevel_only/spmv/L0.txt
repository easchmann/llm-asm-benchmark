Registers (System V)
- nrows arrives in rdi, ncols in rsi, rowptr in rdx, col in rcx, val in r8, x in r9; y is the seventh argument and is on the stack at 8(%rsp) on entry. It is loaded into r10 at the start.
- rdi can count rows; rdx and r10 advance by 8 bytes per row (rowptr[i], y[i]).
- rax holds k, rsi (ncols is not needed) can hold the end position, r11 holds col[k].
- xmm0 holds the running value, xmm1 a temporary.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved and the stack is not otherwise touched.

Memory
- rowptr, col and val entries are 8 bytes: rowptr[i] at rowptr + 8*i, col[k] at col + 8*k, val[k] at val + 8*k, x[c] at x + 8*c.

Vector instructions
- None; the column indices are irregular, so only scalar code is used (VEX-encoded scalar double instructions).
