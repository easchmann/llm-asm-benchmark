Registers (System V)
- tsteps arrives in rdi, n in rsi, A in rdx, B in rcx. Nothing on the stack.
- rdx and rcx can serve as source and destination pointers.
- rbx and r12 are callee-saved and are pushed at entry and popped before returning.
- Row pointers: r10 (source row i), rbx (source row i-1), r12 (source row i+1), r11 (destination row i), row stride 8*n bytes. r8 holds the row index, r9 the column index, rax the end of the vector part, 1 + ((n - 2) & -4).
- ymm15 holds 0.2 broadcast (from a constant in .rodata); ymm0-ymm2 are temporaries.

Memory and tails
- The address of column j is row pointer + 8*j, with -8 and +8 for the left and right neighbours.
- The vector part ends at 1 + ((n - 2) & -4); scalar instructions handle the remaining columns. n = 3: scalar only.
- The vector part never reads beyond column n-1 of a row: its last load starts at most at column n-4.
- Vector and scalar instructions combine the values in the same order.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- Pop r12 and rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate add and multiply instructions.
