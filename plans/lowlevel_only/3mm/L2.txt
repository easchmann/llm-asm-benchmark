Registers (System V)
- ni arrives in rdi, nj in rsi, nk in rdx, nl in rcx, nm in r8, E in r9; A, B, F, C, D and G are on the stack at 8, 16, 24, 32, 40 and 48 bytes above rsp on entry.
- Callee-saved rbx, rbp, r12, r13, r14, r15 are pushed at entry (48 bytes), so A, B, F, C, D, G are then at 56, 64, 72, 80, 88 and 96(%rsp). Pop in reverse order before returning.
- The sizes can stay in rdi, rsi, rdx, rcx, r8 throughout and E in r9.
- Matrix-product registers: rbx a row index, r13, r14, r15 row pointers (stride 8 * row length bytes), r12 a matrix start, rbp an inner index, rax a column index, r10 = row length & -4.
- ymm0 holds a broadcast scalar, ymm1, ymm2 temporaries.

Memory and tails
- Vector parts cover row lengths & -4; scalar tails handle lengths that are not a multiple of 4; vector parts are skipped for rows shorter than 4.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- Pop the registers, vzeroupper, return.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add.
