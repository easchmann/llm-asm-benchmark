Registers (System V)
- m arrives in rdi, n in rsi, A in rdx, B in rcx; alpha in xmm0. Nothing on the stack.
- rbx and r12 are callee-saved, pushed at entry and popped at the end. rbx holds the column index j, r12 = n & -4.
- r9 holds a row index, r8 a row pointer into B (B + 8*n*i).
- r11 holds k, r10 a row pointer into B, rax a pointer to A[k][i] (A + 8*(k*m + i)), whose stride over k is 8*m bytes.
- ymm0 holds alpha broadcast (xmm0 low lane stays alpha), ymm2 a broadcast element of A, ymm3, ymm4 temporaries.

Memory and tails
- Vector parts cover columns below n & -4; scalar parts handle the rest. n < 4 or not a multiple of 4: scalar tails.
- Unaligned vector loads and stores.
- Pop r12 and rbx, vzeroupper, return.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add.
