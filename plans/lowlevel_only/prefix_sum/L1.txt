Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, a in rsi, out in rdx. Nothing on the stack.
- rcx holds the element index, rax the running value, r8 a temporary.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Memory
- a[i] at a + 8*i, out[i] at out + 8*i (64-bit integers).
- 64-bit integer addition; overflow wraps, as in two's complement arithmetic.

Vector instructions
- None; only scalar general-purpose instructions are used.
