Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, L in rsi, x in rdx, y in rcx. Nothing on the stack.
- rsi can serve as the pointer to the current row of L (row stride 8*n bytes).
- r8 holds the row index, r9 the column index, r10 the number of used entries in the row, r11 = r10 & -4.
- ymm0 is a vector accumulator, ymm1 a temporary.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Memory and tails
- L[i][j] is at L + 8*(i*n + j); x[j], y[i] at base + 8*index.
- The vector part stops at a multiple of 4 that is at most the number of used entries; scalar instructions handle the rest.
- The accumulator is reduced to a scalar.
- vzeroupper before returning.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add; four-lane partial sums.
