Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, A in rsi, B in rdx, tmp in rcx, x in r8, y in r9; alpha in xmm0, beta in xmm1. Nothing on the stack.
- rsi and rdx can serve as row pointers of A and B (row stride 8*n bytes).
- r10 holds the row index, r11 the column index, rax = n & -4.
- ymm2 and ymm3 are vector accumulators; ymm4, ymm5 temporaries. xmm0 and xmm1 are kept unchanged.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Memory and tails
- A[i][j] and B[i][j] are at base + 8*(i*n + j); tmp[i], y[i] at base + 8*i.
- Vector parts cover columns below n & -4; scalar instructions handle the last columns; no read beyond column n-1. n < 4: vector parts skipped.
- Accumulators are reduced to scalars (upper half onto lower half, then the two lanes).
- Unaligned vector loads.
- vzeroupper before returning.

Vector instructions
- AVX, 256-bit, separate multiply and add; four-lane sums change the order of additions (results agree up to rounding).
