Registers (System V)
- n arrives in rdi, data in rsi, nbins in rdx, hist in rcx. Nothing on the stack.
- rax holds an index, r8 holds the current data value.
- Only caller-saved registers are used; nothing is saved.

Memory
- data[i] at data + 8*i; hist[b] at hist + 8*b. A zero is stored as a 64-bit value.
- Counters are incremented with scalar 64-bit increments in memory, at addresses that depend on the data.

Vector instructions
- None.
